About the Church

Shalom Vine Church

We are a family church, rooted in the word of God; a welcoming community where every generation finds a place to belong, grow, and thrive.

Anchored in Christ and guided by the truth of Scripture, we cultivate a warm, nurturing environment that strengthens families, builds lasting relationships, and encourages deep spiritual growth.

Whether you are seeking a place to anchor your faith, raise your family in God's love, or discover your true purpose, Shalom Vine International Christian Center offers a vibrant home where faith comes alive and God's peace connects us all.

The Lead Pastor

Learn more about our Global Lead Pastor and his divine mandate.

Apostle Kenneth Eze

Founder & Lead Pastor, Shalom Nation

Apostle Kenneth Eze is the Founder and Lead Pastor of Shalom Vine International Christian Centre (Shalom Nation) in Lagos, Nigeria.

With the divine mandate of "Taking Healing to the World," he is passionate about seeing lives transformed through the power of Jesus Christ. He has preached the Gospel in over 25 states across Nigeria and in several nations, including the United States, South Africa, Kenya, Uganda, Zambia, Liberia, Senegal, Seychelles, and Eswatini.

His ministry is dedicated to bringing healing, hope, and restoration to all who encounter it.
